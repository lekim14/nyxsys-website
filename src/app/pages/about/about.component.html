OUR JOURNEY INTO PROVIDING DIGITAL BUSINESS SOLUTIONS

Founded in June 2014, it wasn't long before Nyxsys Philippines came to be known as a leading provider of digital business solutions. Beginning as a bold spin-off from a well-established Out-Of-Home (OOH) advertising company from the 1990s. Nyxsys has since then transformed into a driving force for digital innovation.

As a team, we believe that digital business solutions are more than just tools: they shape experiences. expertise in Corporate Business Solutions, Digital Signage, and OOH Advertising to deliver next-level results.
